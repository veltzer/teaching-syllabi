How to write a blurb
====================

A blurb (blurbs/*.md) is a one-page description of a short talk or lecture.
It is not a syllabus: it sells a session rather than laying out a course, so
its structure is different. scripts/check_blurb_md.py enforces the format.

- look at "blurbs/ai_ready_documentation.md" to see how a blurb looks like.
- YAML frontmatter with tags, level, category, audience and duration_minutes.
  All values come from the shared-tags vocabulary (shared/shared-tags/); a
  new duration needs a line in duration_minutes.txt there first.
- exactly one <!-- blurb: id --> comment, where id is the file name without .md.
- exactly one title (# ...), followed by a one-paragraph tagline.
- the second-level headers are exactly, in this order:
    ## The Challenge
    ## What This Session Covers
    ## Agenda
    ## Takeaways
    ## Who Should Attend
    ## Continue the Journey      (optional)
    ## Speaker
- every Agenda line is one timed topic:
    * **Topic title** (N min): One sentence on the focus of the topic.
  The topic times are visible to the reader (unlike syllabus chapter
  durations) and must add up to duration_minutes.
- no third-level headers, no nested bullets, no syllabus markers
  (<!-- course: --> / <!-- chapter: -->).
- technical terms follow the shared-terms rules, as in syllabi.
