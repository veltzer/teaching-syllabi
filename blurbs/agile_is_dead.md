# Agile is Dead

**A provocative technical leadership talk.** Most of the ideas behind Agile were answers to
problems that `AI` is making disappear. What is left of Agile when writing code is no longer
the bottleneck?

## The Challenge

Agile was born in 2001 as a response to a specific world: code was expensive to write, slow
to change, and the main constraint on every project was developer time. Sprints, story points,
estimation sessions, stand-ups, and the preference for working software over documentation
were all built around that constraint. `AI` coding agents are removing it. Teams that keep
running the same rituals are optimizing for a bottleneck that no longer exists - while the
real bottlenecks of the `AI` era go ignored.

## What This Session Covers

A deliberately provocative, but practical, re-examination of Agile in the age of `AI`: which of
its ideas were tied to the cost of writing code, which of them `AI` makes obsolete or even
harmful, which ones survive, and what engineering teams should build in their place.

## Agenda

* **The Provocation** (10 min): Why Agile was a brilliant answer to the problems of 2001, and why that is exactly its weakness today.
* **What Agile Was Really Solving** (15 min): Expensive code, slow feedback, and big-batch delivery; the economic assumptions hidden in the Agile Manifesto.
* **When Code Becomes Cheap** (20 min): `AI` agents collapse the cost of implementation; prototypes in hours, rewrites instead of refactors, and what that does to planning.
* **Sprints, Estimation, and Story Points** (20 min): Rituals built around scarce developer time, and why estimating work an agent finishes in minutes is theater.
* **Stand-Ups, Pairing, and Team Size** (15 min): When one engineer orchestrates many agents, coordination, team structure, and the role of the developer change.
* **Documentation Strikes Back** (15 min): "Working software over comprehensive documentation" reversed; specifications and documentation become the primary input to `AI` agents.
* **What Survives, and What Replaces the Rest** (15 min): Customer feedback, iteration, and continuous delivery endure; the new bottlenecks are specification, review, verification, and judgment.
* **Debate and Q&A** (10 min): An open challenge to the audience - defend your favorite Agile practice.

## Takeaways

* A clear view of which Agile practices were tied to the cost of writing code and why `AI` undermines them
* An understanding of the new bottlenecks in `AI`-assisted development
* Concrete ideas for rethinking team processes, planning, and roles for the `AI` era

## Who Should Attend

* Engineering managers
* Tech leads and Scrum masters
* Product and project managers
* Developers who have lived through too many sprint plannings

## Continue the Journey

This talk challenges the old model. A follow-on, hands-on workshop can examine the team's
own process, identify which rituals still earn their keep, and design a workflow built
around `AI` agents.
